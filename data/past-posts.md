## 2026-09-10 · LinkedIn · 134 likes, 27 comments
Last month I asked Jonas why our Colombian tasted flat on the new grinder setting. He said: "Because I changed it and did not write it down."

Two weeks of customers getting a slightly dull cup. Three cafés told us politely. One told us less politely, and he was right.

We fixed it. We also now have a laminated sheet next to the roaster. Every change gets written down, with date and initials. Boring, and it works.

If you run a small team: write the boring thing down. It saves you an awkward phone call. Danke to everyone who told us.

## 2026-09-30 · LinkedIn · 96 likes, 19 comments
Why does a 250 g bag of our coffee cost 11.50 euros?

Green coffee: about 3.40. Roasting, including gas and the 15 percent weight we lose in the drum: 1.60. Packaging and labels: 0.90. Wages, rent, the bike, the insurance: 3.70. Our margin: 1.90. The rest is tax.

We do not discount, because the number above is already tight. Farmers got paid more than the market rate for this lot. I would rather tell you that than put a "SALE" sticker on it.

Ask me anything about the numbers. I like numbers.
