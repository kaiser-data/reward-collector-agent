Fill this page from your agent

In Claude Code, in your agent's folder, type this and press Enter:

! bash .claude/dashboard.sh

Windows: ! powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File .claude/dashboard.ps1

It finds your agent's run logs and test grades and opens this page filled in.
