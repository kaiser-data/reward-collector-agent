An agent that finds leads and drafts the outreach

Every 2 weeks or month: read my contacts, recommend the right course, earn referral rewards. I approve every message.

Status as of 14 Nov 2026

The problem

  • Finding the right contacts and writing to each one by hand takes hours.
  • Follow-ups get forgotten, so rewards are lost.
  • Goal: as automatic as possible, and as many rewards as possible.

My yes
  • Skips anyone contacted in the last 14 days or marked do-not-contact.
  • Matches each lead to a course by their classification.
  • Drafts first messages and follow-ups in one run.

Safety by design

  • Nothing goes out without my yes to the exact text.
  • Reads only local CSVs. No inbox access, no logins.
  • Contact data is private: stays in data/, never in memory or shared files.
  • Never contacts the same person twice in 14 days.
