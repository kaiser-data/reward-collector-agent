# Content plan: Linden Roasters

## Where we post
- LinkedIn: twice a week (Tuesday, Thursday)
- Instagram: three times a week (feed and stories)

## Audience
- Café owners in Berlin
- Office managers choosing coffee for their team
- Coffee nerds who like to taste and ask questions

## Content pillars
1. People: the team, customers, their stories
2. Honest mistakes: what went wrong and what we changed
3. Behind the roasting: how it is actually made
4. Numbers and prices: why coffee costs what it costs

## Never
- No stock photos. Real photos from the roastery or the bike only.
- No "game-changer", no hype words.
- No discount spam.
- No hashtag walls, max 2 emojis.
- No customer name or number without asking first.
